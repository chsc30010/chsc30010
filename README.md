### 🧭 About

I'm a Product Manager who treats AI as a core part of the craft — not a side experiment. Day to day I turn fuzzy problems into shipped products: discovery, strategy, PRDs, launch, growth. Nights and weekends I build the agents, and then I build the harness that tells me whether the agent is actually any good.

That second half is the thread through most of what's below. Demos are easy. Knowing your agent escalates every contact that needed a human, or that a scoring change quietly regressed accuracy by 18 points, is the part that decides whether it ships.

- 🔭 **Building** agentic tooling — support agents, RAG, eval harnesses, PM workflows
- 🧩 **Thinking in** discovery → strategy → execution → launch → growth
- ⚡ **Belief:** an AI feature without an eval is a demo, not a product

### 🚀 Featured Work

**[Omnichannel Customer Support Agent](https://github.com/chsc30010/customer-support-agent)** · `Python`
> Phone, SMS, web chat and email through one pipeline — answers from a knowledge base with citations, and hands over to a human when it shouldn't be the one answering. Across 60 hand-labelled contacts, **every contact that needed a person reached a person**, and it resolved 88% of the rest from the right article. The handover is the point.

**[agenteval — Agentic Eval Harness](https://github.com/chsc30010/agentic-eval-harness)** · `Python`
> Evaluates tool-using agents on *what they did*, not what they said. Records the full trajectory and runs failure-mode detectors over it — tool-error blindness, prompt injection via tool output, hallucinated tools, runaway loops, dropped sub-goals. Dependency-free, exits non-zero, drops straight into CI.

**[Personal RAG](https://github.com/chsc30010/personal-rag)** · `FastAPI · Ollama · ChromaDB`
> Offline-first RAG over your own documents — PDFs, Word, Markdown, code, web pages — with inline citations. LLM and embeddings run locally through Ollama; the vector store is on disk. Nothing leaves the machine.

**[LLM Eval Pipeline](https://github.com/chsc30010/llm-eval-pipeline)** · `Python`
> Provider-agnostic harness for scoring prompt/model outputs: run a JSONL dataset, score each response, get a summary report. New provider = one interface.

**[PM Skills Marketplace](https://github.com/chsc30010/pm-skills)** · `Claude Skills`
> 65 PM skills and 36 chained workflows across 8 plugins, encoding proven frameworks (Teresa Torres, Marty Cagan, Alberto Savoia) into an AI-native workflow — fuzzy idea to launch plan, step by step.
